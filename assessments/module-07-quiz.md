# Module 7 quiz — Which optimizer and parametrization?

This quiz checks the decisions of Module 7 in realistic situations: implementing and testing Muon, choosing which parameters it updates and what it costs, reusing AdamW's hyperparameters through update-RMS matching, controlling attention-logit growth with QK-Clip or QK-norm, transferring learning rates across width with µP, planning schedules with decay branches, and diagnosing a loss spike from its logs. It has 10 multiple-choice questions; the pass mark is 70%.

It covers the five required lessons:

- [07.1 · Muon from scratch](../lessons/module-07/lesson-01.md)
- [07.2 · Muon at scale](../lessons/module-07/lesson-02.md)
- [07.3 · Hyperparameter transfer](../lessons/module-07/lesson-03.md)
- [07.4 · Schedules](../lessons/module-07/lesson-04.md)
- [07.5 · Stability forensics](../lessons/module-07/lesson-05.md)

The module's practical assessment is the [optimizer decision report](../projects/module-07-optimizer-decision.md).

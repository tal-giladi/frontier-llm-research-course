# Module 4 quiz — Does the model use its context?

This quiz checks the decisions of Module 4 in realistic situations: judging what a long-context evaluation does and does not show, validating synthetic tasks, choosing a RoPE rule under a pre-stated guard, planning and budgeting a staged extension, handling document boundaries, and separating the gain of an extension from the effect of extra training tokens. It has 9 multiple-choice questions; the pass mark is 70%.

It covers the three required lessons:

- [04.1 · Long-context evaluation that means something](../lessons/module-04/lesson-01.md)
- [04.2 · Position at long range](../lessons/module-04/lesson-02.md)
- [04.3 · Extending context by continued training](../lessons/module-04/lesson-03.md)

The module's practical assessment is the [context-extension project](../projects/module-04-context-extension.md).

# Module 1 quiz

This quiz checks the decisions Module 1 trains: reading reports and configs as evidence, designing a controlled comparison on a stated axis with an equal tuning budget and a pre-stated rule, separating seed noise from evaluation noise, correcting for several comparisons, sizing an experiment with the minimum detectable effect, and deciding from run cards whether two runs can be compared. Every question is a realistic situation; none asks you to recall wording.

Ten multiple-choice questions; the pass mark is 70%.

Lessons covered:

- [01.1 · Diagnostic and map](../lessons/module-01/lesson-01.md)
- [01.2 · Reading reports and configs as evidence](../lessons/module-01/lesson-02.md)
- [01.3 · Designing an experiment](../lessons/module-01/lesson-03.md)
- [01.4 · Uncertainty](../lessons/module-01/lesson-04.md)
- [01.5 · Reproducibility and the experiment record](../lessons/module-01/lesson-05.md)

The practical assessment of the module is the [Baseline-0 project](../projects/module-01-baseline0.md).

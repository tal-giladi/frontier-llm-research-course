# Module 16 quiz — How do we train agents without fooling ourselves?

This quiz checks the decisions of Module 16 in realistic situations: choosing what a verifier may score and testing it on known-wrong submissions, reading a verifier's error rates honestly, choosing the split unit for generated software-engineering tasks, finding a masking bug in multi-turn RL, reading what a shaped per-turn reward did, deciding whether a run found a loophole, and choosing mitigations for reward hacking that the published evidence supports. It has 8 multiple-choice questions; the pass mark is 70%.

It covers the four required lessons of the module:

- [16.1 · Environments and verifiers](../lessons/module-16/lesson-01.md)
- [16.2 · Software-engineering tasks](../lessons/module-16/lesson-02.md)
- [16.3 · Multi-turn agentic RL](../lessons/module-16/lesson-03.md)
- [16.4 · Reward hacking](../lessons/module-16/lesson-04.md)

The extension lesson [16.5 · Computer-use agents](../lessons/module-16/lesson-05.md) has its own lesson quiz. The module's practical assessment is the [environment-pack project](../projects/module-16-agent-environments.md).

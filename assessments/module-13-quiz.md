# Module 13 quiz — Which post-training pipeline for which target?

This quiz checks the decisions of Module 13 in realistic situations: reading an open post-training recipe for what each stage is for and how strong its evidence is, deriving and stabilising DPO, building preference pairs, judging whether a published stage gain exceeds benchmark noise, choosing between SFT on teacher outputs and on-policy distillation with the teacher's compute counted, training against an AI judge built from a written specification and tracing the judge's errors into the policy, and measuring thinking modes, budgets and routers at equal cost. It has 10 multiple-choice questions on the three required lessons; the pass mark is 70%.

It covers the lessons of the module:

- [13.1 · Open recipes as case studies](../lessons/module-13/lesson-01.md)
- [13.2 · Distillation](../lessons/module-13/lesson-02.md)
- [13.3 · Specification-driven alignment](../lessons/module-13/lesson-03.md)

Lesson [13.4 · Thinking modes and budgets](../lessons/module-13/lesson-04.md) is an extension; it has its own lesson quiz and is not in this one.

The module's practical assessment is the [post-training pipeline project](../projects/module-13-pipeline.md).

# Module 17 quiz — What can we claim about a model's internals?

This quiz checks the decisions of Module 17 in realistic situations: judging a sparse autoencoder by what the model loses when it is spliced in, reading denoising and noising results when components are redundant, choosing controls and held-out prompts that make a patching effect a claim, separating importance from specificity with off-target checks, treating an attribution graph's predictions as hypotheses to test by intervention, and extracting and evaluating a steering vector without confounds or selection on the evaluation data. It has 9 multiple-choice questions; the pass mark is 70%.

It covers the four required lessons of the module:

- [17.1 · Features and sparse autoencoders](../lessons/module-17/lesson-01.md)
- [17.2 · Causal interventions](../lessons/module-17/lesson-02.md)
- [17.3 · Transcoders and attribution graphs](../lessons/module-17/lesson-03.md)
- [17.4 · Steering and persona vectors](../lessons/module-17/lesson-04.md)

The extension [17.5 · Interpretable by design and introspection](../lessons/module-17/lesson-05.md) has its own lesson quiz. The module's practical assessment is the [causal-claim project](../projects/module-17-causal-claim.md).

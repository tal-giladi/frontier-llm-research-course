# Module 5 quiz — When is sub-quadratic attention worth it?

This quiz checks the decisions of Module 5 in realistic situations: judging whether a linear-attention kernel is correct, choosing between a hybrid and learned sparse attention for a stated constraint, training a sparse-attention indexer, reading component profiles and crossovers without over-generalising them, applying the honest cost model of DSA, and deciding what an Eval v1 result can and cannot say about MiniMax's counter-hypothesis. It has 9 multiple-choice questions; the pass mark is 70%.

It covers the three required lessons:

- [05.1 · Linear and hybrid attention at scale](../lessons/module-05/lesson-01.md)
- [05.2 · Learned sparse attention](../lessons/module-05/lesson-02.md)
- [05.3 · Measuring cost and quality honestly](../lessons/module-05/lesson-03.md)

The extension lesson [05.4 · Compressed sparse attention (DeepSeek-V4 CSA/HCA)](../lessons/module-05/lesson-04.md) has its own quiz and is not part of this one. The module's practical assessment is the [sub-quadratic decision memo](../projects/module-05-subquadratic-memo.md).

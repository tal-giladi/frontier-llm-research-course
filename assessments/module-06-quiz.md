# Module 6 quiz — Which other block changes earn their complexity?

This quiz checks the decisions of Module 6 in realistic situations: choosing the comparison axis for a change that adds training FLOPs (multi-token prediction) or wall-clock but few FLOPs (hyper-connections and mHC), reading a null stability result at small scale, explaining what the doubly-stochastic constraint guarantees and what stays approximate, and planning, measuring and reporting the combination of several changes without selection bias. It has 9 multiple-choice questions; the pass mark is 70%.

It covers the three required lessons:

- [06.1 · Multi-token prediction](../lessons/module-06/lesson-01.md)
- [06.2 · Residual-stream design: hyper-connections and mHC](../lessons/module-06/lesson-02.md)
- [06.3 · Combining changes](../lessons/module-06/lesson-03.md)

The extension lessons — [06.4 · Conditional memory and lookup sparsity: Engram](../lessons/module-06/lesson-04.md), [06.5 · Elastic architectures](../lessons/module-06/lesson-05.md), [06.6 · Tokenizer-free models: Byte Latent Transformer](../lessons/module-06/lesson-06.md) and [06.7 · Non-autoregressive and latent reasoning](../lessons/module-06/lesson-07.md) — have their own quizzes and are not part of this one. The module's practical assessment is the [Lineage-F integration experiment](../projects/module-06-lineage-f.md).

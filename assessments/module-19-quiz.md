# Module 19 quiz — How is frontier research chosen, reproduced and written?

This quiz checks the decisions of Module 19 in realistic situations: choosing between proposals by the decision they inform, their chance of a decisive answer and their cost; reading a cheap proxy before trusting it; deciding in advance what counts as reproduced and applying that rule to seed intervals; recording deviations from a paper; checking a write-up's claims, figures and checkpoints against its run cards; and answering a reviewer with numbers. It has 10 multiple-choice questions; the pass mark is 70%.

It covers the three lessons of the module:

- [19.1 · Research taste and problem choice](../lessons/module-19/lesson-01.md)
- [19.2 · Reproducing a paper](../lessons/module-19/lesson-02.md)
- [19.3 · Writing and defending results](../lessons/module-19/lesson-03.md)

The module's practical assessment is the [capstone proposal project](../projects/module-19-capstone-proposal.md).

# Module 18 quiz — Alignment science, frontier evaluation and governance

This quiz checks the decisions of Module 18 in realistic situations: choosing the controls that make a model-organism result interpretable, reading released samples and toy reproductions for what they do and do not show, keeping a monitor useful when training could push against it, scoring monitors honestly, deciding whether a benchmark score can be trusted, quoting a time horizon with its uncertainty, and applying the rule-out logic of the safety frameworks to an audit. It has 10 multiple-choice questions; the pass mark is 70%.

It covers the four lessons of the module:

- [18.1 · Model organisms of misalignment](../lessons/module-18/lesson-01.md)
- [18.2 · Chain-of-thought monitorability](../lessons/module-18/lesson-02.md)
- [18.3 · Frontier evaluation](../lessons/module-18/lesson-03.md)
- [18.4 · Safety frameworks and system cards](../lessons/module-18/lesson-04.md)

The module's practical assessment is the [audit-report project](../projects/module-18-audit-report.md).

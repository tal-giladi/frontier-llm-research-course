# Module 2 quiz — Where does the time go?

This quiz checks the decisions of Module 2 in realistic situations: predicting step time from FLOPs and the roofline, reading a profile and an activation-memory account, measuring exposed communication, and judging whether a performance claim is supported. It has 9 multiple-choice questions; the pass mark is 70%.

It covers the four required lessons:

- [02.1 · From FLOPs to time](../lessons/module-02/lesson-01.md)
- [02.2 · Profiling a training step](../lessons/module-02/lesson-02.md)
- [02.3 · Communication and overlap](../lessons/module-02/lesson-03.md)
- [02.4 · Validating a performance claim](../lessons/module-02/lesson-04.md)

The module's practical assessment is the [performance report on Baseline-0](../projects/module-02-performance-report.md).

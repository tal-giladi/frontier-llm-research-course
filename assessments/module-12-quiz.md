# Module 12 quiz — Post-training foundations

This quiz checks the decisions of Module 12 in realistic situations: testing a verifier before RL uses it, judging and over-optimising learned reward models, choosing baselines and normalisation for group advantages, handling zero-variance groups, placing a KL penalty and choosing its estimator, weighting tokens across responses of different lengths, treating truncated responses, masking the loss, keeping importance ratios honest under staleness and sampler mismatch, and deciding with Eval Suite v2 whether a post-training stage passes. It has 10 multiple-choice questions; the pass mark is 70%.

It covers the four lessons of the module:

- [12.1 · Rewards](../lessons/module-12/lesson-01.md)
- [12.2 · Policy-gradient estimators for LLMs](../lessons/module-12/lesson-02.md)
- [12.3 · Details that change results](../lessons/module-12/lesson-03.md)
- [12.4 · Eval Suite v2](../lessons/module-12/lesson-04.md)

The module's practical assessment is the [correctness-checked RL loop project](../projects/module-12-rl-loop.md).

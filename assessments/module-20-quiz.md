# Module 20 quiz — Capstone: reproduce, extend, defend

This quiz checks the decisions of the capstone in realistic situations: scoping a published claim to what a small run can test, telling "not tested" from "not reproduced", choosing the comparison axis and an equivalence margin before the runs, computing intervals that respect both seeds and evaluation items, keeping claims within evidence, and reviewing, defending and revising a result in the right order. It has 9 multiple-choice questions; the pass mark is 70%.

It covers the two lessons of the module:

- [20.1 · Capstone brief and rubric](../lessons/module-20/lesson-01.md)
- [20.2 · Review and defence](../lessons/module-20/lesson-02.md)

The module's practical assessment is the [capstone project](../projects/module-20-capstone.md).

# Module 8 quiz — How low can precision go?

This quiz checks the decisions of Module 8 in realistic situations: reading FP8, FP4 and scale formats at the bit level, choosing scaling granularity and scale formats, quantising each GEMM operand along the right dimension, handling limited accumulation precision, telling emulated numerics from measured speed, using the ingredients of the NVFP4 recipe, and telling FP4 pretraining from quantisation-aware training and post-training quantisation. It has 9 multiple-choice questions; the pass mark is 70%.

It covers the three required lessons:

- [08.1 · Number formats and scaling](../lessons/module-08/lesson-01.md)
- [08.2 · FP8 training](../lessons/module-08/lesson-02.md)
- [08.3 · FP4 training and quantisation-aware training](../lessons/module-08/lesson-03.md)

The extension lesson [08.4 · Scaling laws for precision](../lessons/module-08/lesson-04.md) has its own quiz and is not part of this one. The module's practical assessment is the [precision plan](../projects/module-08-precision-plan.md).

# Module 10 quiz — Which data, in which mix?

This quiz checks the decisions of Module 10 in realistic situations: keeping provenance and licence records, finding duplicates and benchmark leakage across splits, packing with and without document masking, resuming a mixture exactly, judging a quality filter by a training ablation, counting the cost of synthetic data, choosing mixtures from small runs and short anneals, and measuring forgetting against the right control. It has 10 multiple-choice questions; the pass mark is 70%.

It covers the five required lessons:

- [10.1 · Data integrity at scale](../lessons/module-10/lesson-01.md)
- [10.2 · Model-based quality filtering](../lessons/module-10/lesson-02.md)
- [10.3 · Synthetic and rephrased data](../lessons/module-10/lesson-03.md)
- [10.4 · Mixtures and micro-anneals](../lessons/module-10/lesson-04.md)
- [10.5 · Continued training and forgetting](../lessons/module-10/lesson-05.md)

The extension lesson [10.6 · Multilingual and code data](../lessons/module-10/lesson-06.md) is not part of this quiz. The module's practical assessment is the [Data-v1 project](../projects/module-10-data-v1.md).

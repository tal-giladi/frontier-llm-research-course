# Module 15 quiz — How should compute be spent at inference?

This quiz checks the decisions of Module 15 in realistic situations: comparing ways of spending inference compute at matched budgets in tokens and in latency, telling oracle pass@N from what a selection procedure achieves, charging a verifier's cost to the method that uses it, choosing a draft and a draft length for speculative decoding and predicting when its speed-up survives, and reading what an attention design or a scheduling policy does to serving capacity and to RL rollout time. It has 10 multiple-choice questions; the pass mark is 70%.

It covers the three required lessons of the module:

- [15.1 · A test-time compute experiment](../lessons/module-15/lesson-01.md)
- [15.2 · Speculative decoding](../lessons/module-15/lesson-02.md)
- [15.3 · Serving cost of architecture choices](../lessons/module-15/lesson-03.md)

The extension [15.4 · KV and weight quantisation for serving](../lessons/module-15/lesson-04.md) has its own lesson quiz. The module's practical assessment is the [test-time-compute report project](../projects/module-15-ttc-report.md).

# Module 3 quiz — How should attention spend KV memory?

This quiz checks the decisions of Module 3 in realistic situations: computing and comparing decode-cache sizes, judging MLA and local/global implementations by their correctness checks, choosing a comparison axis for an attention change, reading sink, logit and gating evidence at the right strength, and turning measurements into a serving decision. It has 10 multiple-choice questions; the pass mark is 70%.

It covers the three required lessons:

- [03.1 · Multi-head Latent Attention](../lessons/module-03/lesson-01.md)
- [03.2 · Local/global attention and KV arithmetic](../lessons/module-03/lesson-02.md)
- [03.3 · Logit control, sinks and gating](../lessons/module-03/lesson-03.md)

The extension lesson [03.4 · Head count and head dimension](../lessons/module-03/lesson-04.md) has its own quiz and is not part of this one. The module's practical assessment is the [attention memo](../projects/module-03-attention-memo.md).

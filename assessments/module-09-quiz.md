# Module 9 quiz — What does the cluster cost, and how does it fail?

This quiz checks the decisions of Module 9 in realistic situations: ordering parallel dimensions over a cluster's bandwidth hierarchy, choosing context parallelism and checking ring attention, picking a pipeline schedule from its bubble and memory, reading a multi-GPU measurement against a pre-stated decision rule, recognising a framework feature that runs but does not compose correctly for an architecture, saving and restoring the full training state, and choosing a checkpoint interval as the cluster grows. It has 9 multiple-choice questions; the pass mark is 70%.

It covers the four required lessons:

- [09.1 · Parallelism layouts at scale](../lessons/module-09/lesson-01.md)
- [09.2 · Pipeline schedules and overlap](../lessons/module-09/lesson-02.md)
- [09.3 · A measured multi-GPU investigation](../lessons/module-09/lesson-03.md)
- [09.4 · Failure and recovery](../lessons/module-09/lesson-04.md)

The extension lesson [09.5 · TPUs, JAX and hardware co-design](../lessons/module-09/lesson-05.md) has its own quiz and is not part of this one. The module's practical assessment is the [infrastructure plan](../projects/module-09-infrastructure-plan.md).

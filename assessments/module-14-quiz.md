# Module 14 quiz — Which RL objective, at what scale?

This quiz checks the decisions of Module 14 in realistic situations: reading what an RL objective does to each token's gradient, designing an objective comparison that a reviewer cannot explain away, using a random-reward control, choosing behaviour log-probabilities and a staleness bound for asynchronous rollouts, handling the mismatch between the rollout engine and the trainer, deciding whether a compute-performance curve identifies its asymptote, and reading pass@k curves in the debate about whether RL adds capability. It has 10 multiple-choice questions; the pass mark is 70%.

It covers the four lessons of the module:

- [14.1 · Objectives derived, not switched](../lessons/module-14/lesson-01.md)
- [14.2 · A controlled objective comparison](../lessons/module-14/lesson-02.md)
- [14.3 · Rollout systems and staleness](../lessons/module-14/lesson-03.md)
- [14.4 · RL scaling and the capability debate](../lessons/module-14/lesson-04.md)

The module's practical assessment is the [reasoning-RL project](../projects/module-14-reasoning-rl.md).

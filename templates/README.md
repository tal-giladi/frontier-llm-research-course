# Templates

Documents the labs, projects and the capstone ask for. Copy one into your own notes and fill it in.

- [Experiment contract](experiment-contract.md) — filled in before any comparison: question, baseline, controls, comparison axis, budget, metrics, decision rule, limits.
- [Run card](run-card.md) — what every run records, and what must match between compared runs.
- [Experiment rubric](experiment-rubric.md) — how projects and the capstone are assessed, and the written defence.
